Tangent + LRAM Investigation Guide

Target: about 30 minutes.

Flow: Part A 13–15 min; Part B 13–15 min. If short on time, do A1, A2, B1, then debrief B2 rather than finishing every rectangle.

Part A

  • A1: point $(1,1)$; tangent slope should settle near $2$; line $y-1\approx2(x-1)$.
  • A2: point $(0,3)$; tangent slope should settle near $-1$; line $y-3\approx-x$.
  • Prompt: “What stayed fixed? What changed?” Keep the focus on point + slope, not derivative rules.
  • Common issue: students adjust the point as well as $m$. Return them to $y=m(x-x_1)+y_1$.

Part B

  • B1: $\Delta x=0.8$; left endpoints $0,0.8,1.6,2.4,3.2$.
  • B2: $\Delta x=0.5$; left endpoints $0,0.5,1,1.5,2,2.5,3,3.5$.
  • Approximation quality matters less than correct left-endpoint structure and a defensible graph reading.
  • Prompt: “Where does each rectangle touch the curve?”
Look for: target coordinates, an explicit slope estimate, point-slope form, drawn left rectangles, $\Delta x$, and visible estimation reasoning.
