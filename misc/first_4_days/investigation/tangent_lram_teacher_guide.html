Investigation · Teacher Guide

Reasonable estimates, pacing, misconceptions, and facilitation prompts.

Teacher Resource — answers/facilitation notes are visible on this page.
Suggested flow: 3 min launch → 10 min Part A → 12 min Part B → 5 min compare/debrief. If short on time, complete A1 and B1, then use A2/B2 as optional extensions.
A1: point $(1,1)$; reasonable tangent slope $m\approx2$; $y-1=2(x-1)$.
A2: point $(0,0)$; reasonable tangent slope $m\approx-3$; $y=-3x$.
B1: $\Delta x=0.5$; LRAM $=12.75$. Function is increasing, so the picture should suggest an underestimate.
B2: $\Delta x=0.5$; LRAM $=13.625$. Function is increasing on $[0,4]$, so LRAM is an underestimate.

Facilitation prompts

  • “What are the two things your point-slope equation must know?” → point + slope.
  • “Keep the target point fixed. What happens to the secant slope when the other point moves closer?”
  • “Show me where the left endpoint is for rectangle 5.”
  • “How do you know your rectangle width matches the interval and count?”

Likely misconceptions

  • Using $(x_1,y_1)$ from a nearby point rather than the target point.
  • Changing both secant points while trying to estimate local slope.
  • Using 8 as $\Delta x$ because there are 8 rectangles.
  • Reading heights at right endpoints by habit.
