Primary bank: Practice 2. Do not create a second unrelated question set. Suggested 20–30 minute menu: Whiteboard Partners for P2-1/P2-2, then Showdown for P2-5/P2-6; or run four stations.

What to listen for

  • Students name the target point before writing a tangent-line equation.
  • Students use “closer” to justify a better secant estimate rather than simply choosing a prettier number.
  • Students identify the left endpoint of each rectangle verbally or by marking it.
  • Interpretation items include units and a meaning, not just a number.

Quick answers

P2-1: $m\approx2$.
P2-2: $m\approx4$; $y-3=4(x-1)$.
P2-3: Student B, with normal accuracy checks.
P2-4: $\Delta x=0.5$, LRAM $12.75$.
P2-5: $40$.
P2-6: gallons, accumulated amount.
