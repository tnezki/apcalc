Activity Options · Practice 2 Mathematics

Choose a collaboration structure that changes participation without changing the mathematics.

Activity Source: Use Practice 2 as the question bank. Your teacher will choose one structure or rotate structures. The math stays the same; the participation changes.
Whiteboard Partners

Partner A solves; Partner B coaches

  1. Teacher assigns a Practice 2 question.
  2. Partner A writes the solution on the board while narrating choices.
  3. Partner B may ask questions, point to the graph, and request a check—but does not take the marker.
  4. When both agree, compare with a nearby pair.
  5. Switch roles for the next question.

Best fits: P2-1, P2-2, P2-4.

Rally Coach

One works, one checks the process

  1. Solver completes one step at a time.
  2. Coach checks the target point, slope setup, or LRAM endpoint choice before the solver continues.
  3. If there is disagreement, both return to the graph/table evidence.
  4. Switch roles after each question.

Best fits: P2-1, P2-4, P2-5.

Showdown

Think alone, reveal together

  1. Everyone solves the assigned short question privately.
  2. When the teacher says “showdown,” all boards are revealed at once.
  3. Group members identify one agreement and one difference.
  4. Revise only after explaining the reason for the change.

Best fits: P2-3, P2-5, P2-6.

Stations

Four focused stops

  1. Station 1: P2-1 — nearby secants.
  2. Station 2: P2-2 — tangent line.
  3. Station 3: P2-4 — draw LRAM.
  4. Station 4: P2-6 — interpretation and units.
  5. At each station, leave one visible representation or explanation before rotating.
Quality check before you move on: Does your work show the target point? Does your slope use $\Delta y/\Delta x$ consistently? Does your LRAM use left endpoints? Are units included when the context has units?
