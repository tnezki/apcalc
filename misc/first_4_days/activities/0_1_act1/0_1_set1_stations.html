AP Calculus AB · First 4 Days

Set 1 Stations

Print or project. Students leave a readable setup and explanation at each stop.

Station 1

For $g(x)=x^3$, use $(1,1)$ and $(1.5,3.375)$ to find a secant slope.

Station 2

For $g(x)=x^3$, keep $(1,1)$ fixed and use $(1.1,1.331)$. Find the secant slope.

Station 3

Nearby secant slopes are $3.31,3.15,3.03,3.003$. Estimate the tangent slope at the target point.

Station 4

A curve passes through $(2,-1)$ and tangent slope is estimated as $5$. Write the tangent line in point-slope form.

Station 5

A curve passes through $(-1,2)$ and nearby secant slopes approach $-1.5$. Write an estimated tangent line.

Station 6

Which two pieces of information are required to write $y=m(x-x_1)+y_1$?

Station 7

On $[0,5]$ with 5 equal rectangles, state $\Delta x$ and list the left endpoints.

Station 8

Use LRAM with left heights $3,5,7,6,4$ and $\Delta x=1$. Find the estimate.
