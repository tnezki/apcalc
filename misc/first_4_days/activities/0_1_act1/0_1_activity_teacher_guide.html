AP Calculus AB · First 4 Days · Teacher

Activity — Teacher & Student Moves

Learning evidence

I can estimate instantaneous rate of change from nearby secants.

  • Teacher: ask which point stayed fixed; ask what value the slopes appear to approach.
  • Student: mark the target point; show secant calculations; state a defensible tangent estimate.

I can estimate area with LRAM.

  • Teacher: ask for $\Delta x$ and the left endpoints before arithmetic.
  • Student: mark left endpoints; draw/use left heights; show the rectangle sum.

Participation moves

  • Whiteboards: scan for visible setups before calling on a speaker.
  • Rally Coach: coaches ask, “Which point/endpoint are you using?” rather than giving steps.
  • Showdown: preserve private think time before reveal.
  • Stations: require a readable representation before rotation.
  • Find Someone Who: signatures come only after an explanation the student understands.
