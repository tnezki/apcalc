Problem 1
Transformation language

For $g(x)=a\,f(x-h)+k$, describe what $a$, $h$, and $k$ do to the graph of $y=f(x)$.

AP Calculus AB • First 4 Days • Parent Function TransformationsActivity Set 2
Solution 1
Transformation language

For $g(x)=a\,f(x-h)+k$, describe what $a$, $h$, and $k$ do to the graph of $y=f(x)$.

Solution:

$a$ vertically stretches/compresses the graph by $|a|$ and reflects it across the $x$-axis when $a\lt0$; $h$ shifts the graph horizontally (right when $h\gt0$, left when $h\lt0$); $k$ shifts the graph vertically (up when $k\gt0$, down when $k\lt0$).

Problem 2
Linear parent

Let $f(x)=x$. For $g(x)=-3f(x-2)+4$, identify $a$, $h$, and $k$, then describe the transformations from $f$ to $g$.

AP Calculus AB • First 4 Days • Parent Function TransformationsActivity Set 2
Solution 2
Linear parent

Let $f(x)=x$. For $g(x)=-3f(x-2)+4$, identify $a$, $h$, and $k$, then describe the transformations from $f$ to $g$.

Solution:

$a=-3$, $h=2$, $k=4$: reflect across the $x$-axis, vertically stretch by $3$, shift right $2$, and shift up $4$.

Problem 4

Solution 4
Absolute-value parent

Let $f(x)=|x|$. For $g(x)=-2f(x+1)-3$, identify $a$, $h$, and $k$, then describe the transformations.

Solution:

Rewrite $x+1$ as $x-(-1)$. Thus $a=-2$, $h=-1$, $k=-3$: reflect across the $x$-axis, vertically stretch by $2$, shift left $1$, and shift down $3$.
