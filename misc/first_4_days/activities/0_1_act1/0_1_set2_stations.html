Station 4 Answers • Set 2

Problem 13

When class begins and the bell rings, what should already be happening at your desk?

Answer: Begin the posted warm-up right away and have the day's materials ready.

Problem 14

Our usual section lasts three class days. What is the main job of Day 1, Day 2, and Day 3?

Answer: Day 1: notes and initial practice. Day 2: investigation and continued practice. Day 3: activity/continued practice and evidence such as an exit ticket.

Problem 15

In your own words, what was the learning target for our opening week of Algebra 1?

Answer: Access, organize, and use Algebra 1 course materials while practicing the complete class routine with familiar mathematics.

Problem 16

If you cannot finish a problem, what should you put on your paper so your mathematical thinking is still visible?

Answer: Do not leave it blank. Show what you understand, identify where you became stuck, and explain what you would try next or what you need clarified.
