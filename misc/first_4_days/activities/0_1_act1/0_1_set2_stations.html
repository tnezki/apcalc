AP Calculus AB · First 4 Days

Set 2 Stations

Print or project. Students leave a readable setup and explanation at each stop.

Station 1

Nearby secant slopes are $-4.8,-4.3,-4.08,-4.01$. Estimate the tangent slope.

Station 2

A curve passes through $(3,5)$ and has estimated tangent slope $-2$. Write a tangent-line equation.

Station 3

For $f(x)=x^2+1$, use $(0,1)$ and $(0.5,1.25)$ to find a secant slope.

Station 4

For $f(x)=x^2+1$, keep $(1,2)$ fixed and use $(1.2,2.44)$. Find the secant slope.

Station 5

On $[-2,2]$ with 8 equal rectangles, state $\Delta x$.

Station 6

List the 8 left endpoints for an LRAM on $[-2,2]$.

Station 7

Use LRAM with $\Delta x=2$ and left heights $1,3,6,4$. Find the estimate.

Station 8

Use LRAM on $[1,5]$ with 4 equal rectangles and heights $2,4,3,5$. Find the estimate.
