Set 1 — Questions / Solutions

Project one problem at a time or use as a teacher solution/checking source.

1

For $g(x)=x^3$, use $(1,1)$ and $(1.5,3.375)$ to find a secant slope.

Teacher check: Ask for the target point / slope evidence / left-endpoint evidence as appropriate.

2

Teacher check: Ask for the target point / slope evidence / left-endpoint evidence as appropriate.

3

4

6

7

8

9

12
