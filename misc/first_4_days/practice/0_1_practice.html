0.1 Get Acquainted — Practice Set

Practice 1 — Build the Ideas

1. For $f(x)=x^2$, use $(1,1)$ and $(2,4)$ to find a secant slope.

2. For $f(x)=x^2$, keep $(2,4)$ fixed and use $(2.5,6.25)$. Find the secant slope.

3. Keep $(2,4)$ fixed and use $(2.1,4.41)$. Find the secant slope. What tangent slope is suggested?

4. Nearby secant slopes are $6.2,5.5,5.1,5.02$. Estimate the tangent slope.

5. A curve passes through $(1,3)$ and has estimated tangent slope $2$. Write a tangent-line equation in point-slope form.

6. A curve passes through $(-2,4)$ and nearby secant slopes approach $-3$. Write an estimated tangent-line equation.

7. On $[0,4]$ with 4 equal rectangles, state $\Delta x$ and list the four left endpoints.

8. Use LRAM for $x=0,1,2,3,4$ with heights $2,4,5,3,1$. Estimate the area on $[0,4]$.

9. Use LRAM on $[0,6]$ with 3 equal rectangles and left heights $1,4,3$. Find the estimate.

10. A left Riemann sum on $[2,7]$ uses 5 equal rectangles. State $\Delta x$ and the five left endpoints.

11. Explain in one sentence why moving a second secant point closer helps estimate an instantaneous rate.

12. Explain in one sentence what information a left rectangle uses for its height.

0.1 Get Acquainted — Practice Set

Practice 2 — Parallel Version / Activity Source

1. For $g(x)=x^3$, use $(1,1)$ and $(1.5,3.375)$ to find a secant slope.

2. For $g(x)=x^3$, keep $(1,1)$ fixed and use $(1.1,1.331)$. Find the secant slope.

3. Nearby secant slopes are $3.31,3.15,3.03,3.003$. Estimate the tangent slope at the target point.

4. A curve passes through $(2,-1)$ and tangent slope is estimated as $5$. Write the tangent line in point-slope form.

5. A curve passes through $(-1,2)$ and nearby secant slopes approach $-1.5$. Write an estimated tangent line.

6. Which two pieces of information are required to write $y=m(x-x_1)+y_1$?

7. On $[0,5]$ with 5 equal rectangles, state $\Delta x$ and list the left endpoints.

8. Use LRAM with left heights $3,5,7,6,4$ and $\Delta x=1$. Find the estimate.

9. Use LRAM on $[0,8]$ with 4 equal rectangles and left heights $2,5,4,7$. Find the estimate.

10. A table is sampled every $0.5$ unit from $x=0$ to $x=3$. How many equal subintervals are there?

11. If a positive increasing curve is estimated with LRAM, would you expect the rectangles to sit mostly below or above the curve? Explain visually.

12. Compare: a secant-slope estimate and an LRAM estimate. What is each trying to measure?
