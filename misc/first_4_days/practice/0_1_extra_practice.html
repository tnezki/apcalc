Parallel Practice

1. Nearby secant slopes are $-4.8,-4.3,-4.08,-4.01$. Estimate the tangent slope.

2. A curve passes through $(3,5)$ and has estimated tangent slope $-2$. Write a tangent-line equation.

3. For $f(x)=x^2+1$, use $(0,1)$ and $(0.5,1.25)$ to find a secant slope.

4. For $f(x)=x^2+1$, keep $(1,2)$ fixed and use $(1.2,2.44)$. Find the secant slope.

5. On $[-2,2]$ with 8 equal rectangles, state $\Delta x$.

6. List the 8 left endpoints for an LRAM on $[-2,2]$.

7. Use LRAM with $\Delta x=2$ and left heights $1,3,6,4$. Find the estimate.

8. Use LRAM on $[1,5]$ with 4 equal rectangles and heights $2,4,3,5$. Find the estimate.

9. A curve is decreasing and positive. Sketch or describe why LRAM may overestimate the area.

10. Explain what changes and what stays fixed as a secant line is used to estimate a tangent line.

11. A target point is $(2,7)$ and $m\approx0$. Write the estimated tangent line.

12. Create one check you could use to catch a wrong left-endpoint choice in an LRAM.
