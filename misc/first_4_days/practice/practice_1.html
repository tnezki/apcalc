Practice 1 · Local Slope + LRAM

Parallel practice with generous workspace for graph annotation.

Name Date
Goal: Use graphical representations to estimate local slope and LRAM area. Draw directly on the graphs when helpful.

P1-1 · Nearby secants

For $f(x)=x^2$ at $x=1$, compute secant slopes from $P=(1,1)$ to points at $x=2$, $1.5$, and $1.1$. Estimate the tangent slope.

P1-2 · Point + slope → tangent line

For $g(x)=x^2-2$ at $x=-1$, use nearby secants to estimate the tangent slope. Then write an estimated tangent-line equation in point-slope form.

P1-3 · Read the local story

At a point on a curve, nearby secant slopes are $2.8,2.95,3.02,3.01$. Give a reasonable tangent-slope estimate and explain why.

P1-4 · Draw LRAM

On $[0,4]$, draw 4 left rectangles under $f(x)=x^2$. State $\Delta x$, list the left-endpoint heights, and compute the LRAM estimate.

P1-5 · LRAM from a table

Use 4 left rectangles on $[0,4]$.

P1-6 · Check the picture

If an increasing positive function is estimated with LRAM, would you generally expect the rectangles to lie mostly below or above the graph? Explain from the picture, not from a memorized rule.
