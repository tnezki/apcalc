Goal: Another full-strength learning opportunity with fresh values.

EP-1 · Nearby secants

For $f(x)=x^2-4x+5$ at $x=1$, compute secant slopes to $x=2$, $1.5$, and $1.1$. Estimate the tangent slope.

EP-2 · Tangent-line estimate

For $g(x)=x^3$ at $x=-1$, use nearby secants to estimate the tangent slope, then write an estimated tangent line through $(-1,-1)$.

EP-3 · Stability check

Secant slopes near a target point are $-4.8,-4.3,-4.12,-4.04$. Give a tangent-slope estimate and describe the evidence.

EP-4 · Draw LRAM

On $[0,6]$, draw 6 left rectangles under $f(x)=\frac14x^2+1$. State $\Delta x$ and compute the estimate.

Use 4 left rectangles on $[0,6]$.

EP-6 · Interpret

A graph shows velocity in meters per second versus time in seconds. What does an LRAM estimate of area represent? Include units.
