Practice 2 · Local Slope + LRAM

A true parallel version used as the main source for the collaboration activity.

Name Date
Goal: Build confidence with the same two ideas using fresh values and graphs.

P2-1 · Nearby secants

For $f(x)=\frac12x^2+1$ at $x=2$, compute secant slopes to $x=3$, $2.5$, and $2.1$. Estimate the tangent slope.

P2-2 · Tangent-line estimate

For $g(x)=x^2+2x$ at $x=1$, estimate the tangent slope from nearby secants and write the tangent-line equation through $(1,3)$.

P2-3 · Compare estimates

Two students estimate the tangent slope at the same target point. Student A uses secants with second points 2 units and 1 unit away. Student B uses second points 0.2 and 0.05 units away. Whose estimate would you trust more, and what would you still check?

P2-4 · Draw LRAM

On $[0,4]$, draw 8 left rectangles under $f(x)=\frac12x^2+1$. State $\Delta x$ and compute the LRAM estimate.

P2-6 · Interpret

Your LRAM value is $42$ for a rate measured in gallons per minute over time measured in minutes. What units should the area estimate have, and what could it represent?
