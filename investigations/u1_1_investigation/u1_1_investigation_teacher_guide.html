The Case of the Missing Representations

Match-up + modeling · designed for 20 minutes

Time20 minutes
Groups2–3 students
Core ideaMultiple Representations of Relationships

Why This Investigation Fits Section 1.1

Students have completed the notes on inputs, outputs, starting values, repeated changes, and moving among context/table/equation/graph representations. This investigation asks them to coordinate those representations and use evidence to detect matches.

Materials Per Group

  • Student investigation
  • Pencil
  • Scissors only if physically cutting the cards

Before Class

  1. Decide whether students will cut cards or record card labels.
  2. If projecting a launch, show a simple table and ask what story/equation could match.

Suggested Flow

6-16Missing representationsPress students to preserve meaning across forms.

Challenge 1: $y=2x+5$.

Challenge 2: Any coherent context with starting value 10 and repeated change 5.

Challenge 3: heights 6, 10, 14, 18; equation $y=4x+6$.

Challenge 4: graph represents $y=16-2x$ on the shown interval; contexts/tables should preserve start 16 and change $-2$.

Watch for: students matching by shared numbers while swapping the meaning of starting value and repeated change.

Strong Synthesis

Students should state that representations match when they describe the same quantities and produce the same input-output pattern. Good explanations cite both the output at $x=0$ and the repeated change.
