Convince Me: equivalent, not equivalent, equivalent, not equivalent, equivalent, not equivalent.

Which One Doesn't Belong? mathematically, $3x+4$ is the only expression not equivalent to the other three. Alternate structural answers may be accepted if clearly defended, then return to equivalence.

Watch for: partial distribution, combining unlike terms, or deciding equivalence from appearance alone.

Strong Synthesis

Equivalent expressions have the same value for every valid input; properties and structure provide a general argument, while substitution can test or find a counterexample.
