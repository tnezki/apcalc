1.

Evaluate $\lim_{x\to-2}(2x^3-5x+1)$.

  1. Use direct substitution.
  2. Explain why no algebraic repair is needed after substitution.
2.

Evaluate $\lim_{x\to5}\frac{x^2-25}{x-5}$.

  1. Identify the indeterminate form from direct substitution.
  2. Factor and simplify.
  3. Evaluate the resulting limit.
3.

Evaluate $\lim_{x\to0}\frac{\sqrt{x+9}-3}{x}$.

  1. Explain why direct substitution is inconclusive.
  2. Use a conjugate to rewrite the expression.
  3. Find the limit.
4.

Suppose $\lim_{x\to2}f(x)=3$ and $\lim_{x\to2}g(x)=-1$.

  1. Find $\lim_{x\to2}[4f(x)+2g(x)]$.
  2. Find $\lim_{x\to2}f(x)g(x)$.
  3. Name the limit laws used.
5.

Near $x=0$, a function $h$ satisfies $-2x^2\le h(x)\le 3x^2$.

  1. Find the limits of both bounding functions.
  2. Use the Squeeze Theorem to find $\lim_{x\to0}h(x)$.
  3. Explain why the two unequal formulas can still squeeze to the same result.

7.

A table contains only inputs greater than $x=4$ and its outputs approach $7$.

  1. Which one-sided limit can the table estimate?
  2. What additional evidence is required before making a two-sided-limit claim?
8.

  1. Find the two-sided limit.
  2. State $f(-2)$.
  3. Explain why the point value does not change the limit.
9.

Right-hand values near $x=-3$ are $8.4,8.04,8.004$ as inputs move closer to $-3$.

  1. Estimate the right-hand limit.
  2. Write it in notation.
  3. State what the superscript $+$ means.
10.

Two representations appear to disagree about a limit. A graph shows a left-hand approach of $1$, while a right-side table approaches $1$.

  1. What can be concluded if both representations describe the same function?
  2. What should be checked before trusting the conclusion?
