  2. The left- and right-hand limits must both exist and be equal.

Station 1 Answers

Question 1

Left $-2$; right $1$; two-sided limit DNE.
Rationale: Trace the graph toward the target x-value separately from the left and from the right. A two-sided limit exists only when those approached y-values agree.

Question 2

Left 2; right 2; two-sided 2.
Rationale: Compare values from inputs on both sides of the target and identify the single output value they approach, rather than relying on the row at the target alone.

Question 3

B.
Correct choice: B. The left- and right-hand limits must both exist and be equal.
Rationale: A finite two-sided limit exists only when the left-hand and right-hand limits both exist and are equal.
