Teacher & Student Moves Guide

Use across the Activity options
I-Can Moves
I Can 1: Determine and interpret average and instantaneous rates of change.

Teacher Moves

  • Ask students to name the evidence or definition they are using.
  • Press for correct notation, units, or theorem hypotheses where appropriate.

Student Moves

  • Show the setup before simplifying.
  • Compare reasoning and revise when the evidence does not support the claim.
I Can 2: Use a limit of difference quotients to determine the slope of a curve.

Student Moves

Student Moves

Participation-Structure Moves
Across structures

Teacher Moves

  • Protect individual think time.
  • Listen for reasoning, not answer copying.
  • Select useful contrasting strategies for discussion.

Student Moves

  • Explain a mathematical move before asking for confirmation.
  • Use notation, representations, and units as evidence.
  • Revise openly when a partner exposes a stronger method.

Whiteboard Indy

Rates of Change and Tangent Lines
← Activity Options
Structure

Individual → independent practice

Setup

Each student gets a whiteboard and one marker.

Directions
  1. Notes are welcome.
  2. Try something before asking for an answer.
  3. Write large and clearly.
  4. Fix mistakes and discuss what changed.
Goal: Individual accountability + low-stakes problem entry
Question sets

Set 1 · Set 2

Looks Like Success
  • Students show visible mathematical reasoning.
  • Partners use definitions, evidence, and precise notation.
  • Errors are revised after discussion.
Doesn’t Look Like
  • Copying an answer without explanation.
  • One student doing all the thinking.
  • Moving on while a disagreement is unresolved.

Rally Coach

Rates of Change and Tangent Lines
← Activity Options
Structure

Partner explanation + alternating roles

Setup

Partners share a workspace; one explains while one records.

Directions
  1. Partner A explains and Partner B records.
  2. Coach with questions, not answers.
  3. Switch roles after each problem.
  4. Both partners verify the final response.
Goal: Verbal reasoning + procedural accuracy

Round Table

Rates of Change and Tangent Lines
← Activity Options
Structure

Team rotation of written reasoning

Setup

Directions
  1. One student starts the solution.
  2. Pass after a meaningful step.
  3. Next student checks before adding.
  4. Continue until the group can defend the result.
