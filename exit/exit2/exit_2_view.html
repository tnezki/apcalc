Section 2.1

Rates of Change and Limits

Ticket A

Q1

Evaluate algebraically: \(\displaystyle\lim_{x\to 12}\frac{x^2-144}{x-12}\). Show the simplification that makes the limit clear.

Q2

For position \(s(t)=t^2+2t\), average velocities from \(t=1\) to \(t=1+0.5,1+0.1,1+0.01\) are \(4.5, 4.1, 4.01\). What instantaneous velocity at \(t=1\) do these values suggest? Explain the trend.

Q3

Let \(g(x)=4x+1\) for \(x\ne 3\), but \(g(3)=0\). Find \(\displaystyle\lim_{x\to 3}g(x)\) and \(g(3)\), then state why the two values may differ.

Q4

A function satisfies \(\displaystyle\lim_{x\to -2^-}f(x)=-4\) and \(\displaystyle\lim_{x\to -2^+}f(x)=2\). Determine whether \(\displaystyle\lim_{x\to -2}f(x)\) exists and identify the reason.
