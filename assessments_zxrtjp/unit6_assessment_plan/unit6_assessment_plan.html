AP Calculus AB — Unit 6: Integration and Accumulation

Essential Standard / Enduring Understanding

Definite integrals measure accumulation and signed change, Riemann sums approximate that accumulation, and the Fundamental Theorem connects accumulation, derivatives, and antiderivatives through strategic integration methods.

  • definite integral

Key Representations

  • rate graphs
  • geometric regions
  • definite integral notation
  • units

Common Misconceptions / Failure Points

  • Treating all geometric area as positive when the integral is signed.
  • Confusing accumulated change with final value when an initial value is needed.
  • Dropping units when integrating a rate.

Unit Themes / Throughlines

Unit Themes / Throughlines
  • Interpret integrals as accumulation with units and sign.
  • Connect numerical approximations, geometry, graphs, and symbolic integrals.
  • Use the Fundamental Theorem to link derivative and integral processes.
  • Maintain distinction between definite values and antiderivative families.
  • Select integration strategies by recognizing structure and chain-rule reversal.
