RulesSix Basic Trig Derivatives
$(\sin x)'=\cos x$$(\cos x)'=-\sin x$$(\tan x)'=\sec^2x$$(\cot x)'=-\csc^2x$$(\sec x)'=\sec x\tan x$$(\csc x)'=-\csc x\cot x$
ReasoningWhy Sine and Cosine Matter

The limit definition plus the fundamental trig limits gives $(\sin x)'=\cos x$ and $(\cos x)'=-\sin x$. The other four rules can be derived from quotient identities.

StrategyChoose the Algebra Rule First

Before differentiating a trig expression, identify whether it is a sum, product, quotient, or simple trig function. The product rule is a common place to make errors.

Example 1 · Basic trig derivatives

Differentiate $y=3\sin x-4\cos x+2\tan x$.

Example 2 · Product rule with trig

Differentiate $f(x)=x^2\sin x$.

Example 3 · Quotient rule with trig

Differentiate $g(x)=\dfrac{\cos x}{x}$.

Example 4 · Tangent line

Find the tangent line to $y=\sec x$ at $x=0$.

Example 5 · Harmonic motion

A spring has position $s(t)=5\sin t$. Find velocity and acceleration and describe the motion at $t=\pi/4$.
