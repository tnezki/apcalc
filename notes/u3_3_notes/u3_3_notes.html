RulesBasic Algebraic Rules

For differentiable functions $u$ and $v$ and constant $c$:

$$\frac{d}{dx}(c)=0,\qquad \frac{d}{dx}(x^n)=nx^{n-1},$$ $$\frac{d}{dx}[cu]=cu',\qquad (u\pm v)'=u'\pm v'.$$
RulesProduct and Quotient

Products and quotients do not differentiate term-by-term.

$$ (uv)'=u'v+uv', $$ $$\left(\frac{u}{v}\right)'=\frac{vu'-uv'}{v^2},\qquad v\ne0.$$
NotationHigher-Order Derivatives

The second derivative is the derivative of the first derivative:

$$f''(x)=\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}.$$

Continue differentiating for $f^{(3)}$, $f^{(4)}$, and beyond.

Example 1 · Rewrite before differentiating

Find $f'(x)$ for $f(x)=5x^4-3x^{-2}+7\sqrt{x}$.

Example 2 · Tangent line

Find the equation of the tangent line to $f(x)=4x^3-6x+5$ at $x=2$.

Example 3 · Product rule

Differentiate $h(x)=(x^2+1)(2x-5)$ two ways: first by expanding, then by using the product rule.

Example 4 · Quotient rule

Differentiate $g(x)=\dfrac{x^2+1}{x-3}$.

Example 5 · Higher-order derivative

Find $f^{(4)}(x)$ for $f(x)=-5x^8+2x^6-9x^3+32x-1$.
