3.2 · Differentiability — Exercises

Name

Period

Show work and use correct derivative notation. Unless a question explicitly says otherwise, work without a calculator.

Foundation
1.Classify the nondifferentiability of $f(x)=|x-2|$ at $x=2$: corner, cusp, vertical tangent, or discontinuity. Justify using one-sided derivatives.
Foundation
2.Explain why $f(x)=x^{2/3}$ is continuous but not differentiable at $x=0$.
Foundation
3.Explain why $f(x)=x^{1/3}$ has a vertical tangent at $x=0$.
Practice
4.Determine whether $f(x)=\begin{cases}x+1,&x<0\\x^2+1,&x\ge0\end{cases}$ is continuous and differentiable at $0$.
Practice
5.Find $a$ and $b$ so that $f(x)=\begin{cases}ax+b,&x<1\\x^2+1,&x\ge1\end{cases}$ is continuous and differentiable at $x=1$.
Practice
6.A function is differentiable at $x=5$. List three facts that must be true about limits or continuity at $x=5$.
Practice
7.Give an example of a function that is continuous at $x=0$ but not differentiable there, and explain why.
AP / Interpretation
8.If $f$ is not continuous at $x=c$, what can you conclude about differentiability at $c$? State the logical principle you are using.
AP / Interpretation
9.A graph has left-hand slope approaching $+\infty$ and right-hand slope approaching $-\infty$ at $x=2$. Name the feature and decide whether a derivative exists.
AP / Interpretation
10.A graph has left- and right-hand derivative limits both equal to $-4$. What additional condition must hold before you may conclude $f'(c)=-4$?
