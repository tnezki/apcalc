AP Calculus AB · Textbook Section 2.2

Limits Involving Infinity · Practice 1

Intro

1. Explain what \(x\to\infty\) means in a limit statement and how a finite limiting value relates to a horizontal asymptote.

2. Why does \(\lim_{x\to\infty}\sin x\) not exist?

3. Evaluate \(\lim_{x\to\infty}\frac{\cos x}{x}\).

4. For positive integer k, determine \(\lim_{x\to\infty}\frac{x^k}{e^x}\).

5. Evaluate \(\lim_{x\to\infty}\frac{3x-2}{2x^2+1}\) and \(\lim_{x\to-\infty}\frac{3x-2}{2x^2+1}\).

6. Evaluate \(\lim_{x\to\infty}\frac{e^{-x}}{x}\).

7. Find the two end limits of \(f(x)=x/|x|\).

8. Evaluate \(\lim_{x\to-3^-}\frac1{x+3}\).

Review

9. Evaluate \(\lim_{x\to(\pi/2)^+}\sec x\).

10. Evaluate \(\lim_{x\to\pm\infty}\frac{\sin x}{2x^2+x}\).

11. Find all vertical asymptotes of \(f(x)=1/(x^2-4)\).

12. For \(f(x)=1/(x^2-4)\), describe the one-sided behavior at x=2.

13. Find all vertical asymptotes of \(f(x)=\frac{1-x}{2x^2-5x-3}\).

14. Give a power-function end-behavior model for \(3x^2-2x+1\). Does it have a horizontal asymptote?

15. Find an end-behavior model and horizontal asymptote for \(\frac{x-2}{2x^2+3x-5}\).

16. Find the horizontal asymptote of \(\frac{3x^2-x+5}{x^2-4}\).

Mastery

17. For \(f(x)=1/x\) when x<0 and f(x)=-1 when x≥0, find the limits as x→-∞, ∞, 0-, and 0+.

18. For \(f(x)=\frac{x-2}{x-1}\) when x≤0 and \(f(x)=1/x^2\) when x>0, find the limits as x→-∞, ∞, 0-, and 0+.

19. Determine \(\lim_{x\to\infty}\frac{\sqrt{x^2+1}}{x}\) and \(\lim_{x\to-\infty}\frac{\sqrt{x^2+1}}{x}\).

20. For \(f(x)=2/(x-5)^2\), identify the vertical asymptote and justify the behavior from both sides.

21. What is \(\lim_{x\to\infty}\frac{4x^2+1}{2x^2-3}\)?

  1. 0
  2. 1
  3. 2
  4. Does not exist

22. Find the slant asymptote of \(f(x)=\frac{x^2+1}{x-1}\).

23. For \(f(x)=\frac{2x+1}{x-3}\), identify the vertical and horizontal asymptotes and determine the one-sided infinite limits at the vertical asymptote.

24. Compare the end behavior of \(x+e^{-x}\) as x→∞ with a simpler model.
