AP Calculus AB · Textbook Section 2.2

Limits Involving Infinity · Extra Practice

Intro

1. If \(\lim_{x\to3^-}f(x)=-\infty\), what graphical feature is suggested at x=3?

2. If \(\lim_{x\to\infty}f(x)=5\), what horizontal asymptote is indicated?

3. Evaluate \(\lim_{x\to\infty}\frac{7x+2}{x^2+1}\).

4. Evaluate \(\lim_{x\to\infty}\frac{4x^2-1}{2x^2+5}\).

5. Determine the vertical asymptote of \(1/(x-6)\).

6. Determine \(\lim_{x\to6^-}1/(x-6)\).

7. Select the best first procedure for \(\lim_{x\to\infty}\frac{3x^2+x}{5x^2-2}\): factor a finite zero, compare leading powers, rationalize, or use a table only.

8. A rational function has degree 2 in the numerator and degree 3 in the denominator. State its end-behavior limit, assuming leading coefficients are nonzero.

Review

9. For \(f(x)=\frac{x+1}{x-2}\), state \(\lim_{x\to2^-}f(x)\) and \(\lim_{x\to2^+}f(x)\).

10. Does a vertical asymptote require both one-sided limits to be the same infinity? Explain.

11. Find the horizontal asymptote of \(f(x)=\frac{6x^3-x}{2x^3+7}\).

12. A function has \(\lim_{x\to\infty}f(x)=2\) and \(\lim_{x\to-\infty}f(x)=-3\). How many horizontal asymptotes can its graph have from this information?

13. Evaluate \(\lim_{x\to\infty}\frac{\sin x}{x}\) and justify without relying on a graph.

14. For \(f(x)=\frac{x^2-9}{x-3}\), is x=3 a vertical asymptote? Explain.

15. Determine all asymptotes of \(f(x)=\frac{2x+5}{x-1}\).

16. Explain why a horizontal asymptote can be crossed by a graph.

Mastery

17. A rational function \(f(x)=\frac{x^2+ax+1}{x^2-4}\) has a removable discontinuity at x=2. Determine a.

18. For \(f(x)=\frac{3x^2+1}{x^2-4}\), justify the horizontal asymptote and identify all vertical asymptotes.

19. A function satisfies \(\lim_{x\to1^-}f(x)=+\infty\) and \(\lim_{x\to1^+}f(x)=-\infty\). State a conclusion that is guaranteed.

20. Evaluate \(\lim_{x\to\infty}(\sqrt{x^2+4x}-x)\).

21. Determine \(\lim_{x\to-\infty}\frac{5x}{\sqrt{x^2+1}}\).

22. A student says \(\lim_{x\to\infty}\frac{x^2}{x}=0\) because the denominator contains x. Diagnose the error.

23. Find a slant asymptote of \(f(x)=\frac{x^2+3x+1}{x+1}\).

24. Construct a rational function with vertical asymptote x=2 and horizontal asymptote y=3.
