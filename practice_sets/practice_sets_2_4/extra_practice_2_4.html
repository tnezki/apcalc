Rates of Change and Tangent Lines · Extra Practice

Intro

1. Find the average rate of change of \(f(x)=x^2\) on [1,4].

2. Write a difference quotient for average rate of change from x=a to x=a+h.

3. Interpret a positive average rate of change of 12 gallons/minute in context.

4. Write a limit that represents f′(4).

5. If f′(3)=5, what is the slope of the tangent line at x=3?

6. If the tangent slope at x=2 is 0, what geometric feature occurs?

7. Estimate f′(2) if f(1.9)=4.61 and f(2.1)=5.41.

8. Which units belong to a derivative if f is measured in meters and x in seconds?

Review

9. For \(f(x)=x^2+1\), evaluate the derivative definition at x=2.

10. For \(f(x)=1/x\), determine f′(1) from a difference quotient.

12. Explain why a secant slope over [a,a+h] can estimate a tangent slope when h is small.

13. A position function has derivative -6 at t=4. Interpret the sign and units.

14. Write tangent-line equation form at x=a using f(a) and f′(a).

15. If f′(a) does not exist because left and right difference-quotient limits disagree, what can be concluded?

16. A function is continuous at a point but has a corner there. Is it differentiable? Explain.
