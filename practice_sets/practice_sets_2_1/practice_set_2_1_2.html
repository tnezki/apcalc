Rates of Change and Limits · Practice 2 · Activity Set 1 Review

Intro

1. A classmate says the only way to investigate a limit is algebra. Name two non-algebraic ways to gather evidence for a limit.

2. Direct substitution gives \(0/0\). Name three legitimate next moves that could rewrite the expression before trying substitution again.

3. An object has position \(x(t)=3t^2+5t\). Find its average velocity from \(t=1\) to \(t=3\).

4. For \(x(t)=3t^2+5t\), determine the instantaneous velocity at \(t=3\) using a limit of average velocities.

5. A two-sided limit fails. Give one finite mismatch behavior, one unbounded behavior, and one oscillatory behavior that could explain the failure.

6. Evaluate \(\lim_{x\to-1}(2x^2-x+4)\).

7. Evaluate \(\lim_{x\to2}\frac{x^2+5}{x+1}\).

8. Evaluate \(\lim_{x\to1}(x+7)^{2/3}\).
