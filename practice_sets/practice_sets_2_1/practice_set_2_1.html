AP Calculus AB · Textbook Section 2.1

Rates of Change and Limits · Practice 1

Intro

1. Describe two ways to investigate whether \(\lim_{x\to c}f(x)\) appears to exist.

2. List four algebraic procedures that may be useful for evaluating a limit when direct substitution is not enough.

3. An object has position \(x(t)=5t^2+7t\). Find its average velocity from \(t=2\) to \(t=4\).

4. For \(x(t)=5t^2+7t\), determine the instantaneous velocity at \(t=4\) using a limit of average velocities.

5. Give three distinct behaviors that can cause a two-sided limit to fail to exist.

6. Evaluate \(\lim_{x\to2}(x^2+3x-1)\).

7. Evaluate \(\lim_{x\to3}\frac{x^2+1}{x+2}\).

8. Evaluate \(\lim_{x\to-2}(x-6)^{2/3}\).

Review

9. Evaluate \(\lim_{x\to1}\frac{x^2-1}{x-1}\).

10. Evaluate \(\lim_{t\to2}\frac{t^2-3t+2}{t^2-4}\).

11. Evaluate \(\lim_{x\to0}\frac{\sqrt{4+x}-2}{x}\).

12. Evaluate \(\lim_{x\to0}\frac{(2+x)^3-8}{x}\).

13. Evaluate \(\lim_{x\to0}\frac{\sin(2x)}{x}\).

14. Evaluate \(\lim_{x\to0}\frac{\sin x}{2x^2-x}\).

15. Evaluate \(\lim_{x\to0}\frac{\sin^2 x}{x}\).

16. Evaluate \(\lim_{x\to0}\frac{3\sin(4x)}{\sin(3x)}\).

Mastery
