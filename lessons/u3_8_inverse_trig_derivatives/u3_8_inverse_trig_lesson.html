Learning Focus & Vocabulary

Learning focus

By the end of this lesson, I can …

  • Differentiate inverse trigonometric functions using the correct rule and the Chain Rule.
  • Explain why the inverse sine derivative follows from implicit differentiation.
  • Find the derivative of an inverse function at a point without solving for its inverse.
  • Recognize when a derivative is undefined or a rule requires a domain restriction.
Vocabulary to highlight
inverse functionUndo a one-to-one function; inputs and outputs switch.
inverse trigAn angle returned from a trig ratio, with restricted range.
principal rangeChosen output interval making the inverse single-valued.
arcsin / arctanNotation for inverse sine and inverse tangent.
reciprocal slopesDerivatives of inverses at corresponding points.
implicit differentiationDifferentiate a relationship without isolating a variable.
