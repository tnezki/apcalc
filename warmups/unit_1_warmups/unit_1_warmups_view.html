Section 1.1

What Is a Limit?

Day 1Algebra readiness · quadratic solving

1

Solve by factoring: \(x^2-7x+12=0\).

Try without a calculator first. Use a calculator if you need one.

2

Solve by completing the square: \(x^2+6x-7=0\).

3

Solve exactly: \(x^2-6x+7=0\). Give the irrational solutions in exact form.

4

Solve exactly: \(x^2+4x+13=0\). Give the complex solutions in \(a+bi\) form.
