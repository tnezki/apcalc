No CalculatorRecent Review · 3.2 Differentiability
Let $f(x)=\begin{cases}kx^2, & x\le 1 \\ 2x+k-2, & x>1.\end{cases}$ For which value of $k$ is $f$ differentiable at $x=1$?
  1. $-1$
  2. $0$
  3. $1$
  4. $2$
  5. No value of $k$ makes $f$ differentiable.
Calculator PermittedSpiral Review · 2.3 Continuity
For $x\ne 0$, let $g(x)=\dfrac{\sqrt{x+9}-3}{x}$. Use a graph or numerical table near $x=0$ to determine which value of $g(0)$ would make $g$ continuous.
  1. $0$
  2. $\dfrac{1}{12}$
  3. $\dfrac{1}{6}$
  4. $\dfrac{1}{3}$
  5. No value makes $g$ continuous.
