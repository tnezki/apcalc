Same Relationship, Different Outfit

Projected curiosity hook · designed for 5–8 minutes

Time5–8 minutes
FormatWhole-class projection
PurposeBefore notes

Why This Demo Fits Section 1.1

The current section asks students to connect contexts, tables, equations, and graphs and justify matches using values, starting values, and repeated changes. This demo creates that need before the notes name the representations formally.

Materials

  • Projected demo only
  • Optional mini-whiteboards for A/B votes

Before Class

  1. Open the demo at Slide 1.
  2. Plan to withhold the equation choice until students have described the table.

Suggested Flow

MinutesPhaseTeacher Focus
0-2Notice the tableAsk for two features: output at input 0 and repeated change.
2-4Choose a contextRequire evidence, not a guess.
4-6Choose/check equationAsk how substitution or a quick table check could verify the match.
6-7BridgeLeave the graph question unresolved.

Expected Student Thinking

Students should notice a start of 5 and an increase of 3. Some will reverse those numbers in the equation; that is productive tension for the notes.

Watch for: students matching by seeing the same numbers rather than preserving what each number means.

Transition Into Notes

“If one relationship can wear several different outfits, what evidence proves the outfits belong together?”
