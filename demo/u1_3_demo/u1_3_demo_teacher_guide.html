Four Patterns Walk Into a Room

Projected curiosity hook · designed for 5–8 minutes

Time5–8 minutes
FormatWhole-class projection
PurposeBefore notes

Why This Demo Fits Section 1.3

The current section recognizes linear, quadratic, exponential, and absolute value families from evidence across representations. The demo asks students to describe behavior before attaching family names.

Materials

  • Projected demo only

Suggested Flow

MinutesPhaseTeacher Focus
0-2A vs BContrast repeated addition with repeated multiplication.
2-4Pattern CNotice symmetry and changing differences.
4-6Pattern DCompare two symmetric tables without naming families yet.
6-8BridgeAsk what evidence besides graph shape can classify a family.

Expected Student Thinking

Students may say C and D “look the same” because both are symmetric. That sets up the need for stronger evidence from table pattern and equation structure.

Watch for: naming a family from a single visual clue with no supporting evidence.

Transition Into Notes

“If two graphs are both symmetric, what other evidence can tell us which family each belongs to?”
