The Sealed Bag

Projected curiosity hook · designed for 5–8 minutes

Time5–8 minutes
FormatWhole-class projection
PurposeBefore notes

Why This Demo Fits Section 1.5

The section treats equations as balanced models of situations, then connects solution steps, checking, and interpretation. The sealed-bag balance gives the equation a concrete meaning without becoming the later investigation.

Materials

  • Projected demo
  • Optional two identical opaque cups/bags and counters for a physical version

Before Class

  1. If doing the physical version, place 9 counters in each identical container and show 5 loose counters on the same side as the containers.

Suggested Flow

MinutesPhaseTeacher Focus
0-2PredictAsk for a reasonable value before solving.
2-4Model choiceConnect each term to the balance story.
4-6Interpret/checkAsk what 9 means and how substitution confirms it.
6-7BridgeFrame equations as models, not isolated puzzles.

Expected Student Thinking

The correct model is $2x+5=23$, giving $x=9$. The check is $2(9)+5=23$.

Watch for: choosing $2(x+5)=23$, which would mean each bag comes with 5 additional counters.

Transition Into Notes

“How can we build an equation that preserves the meaning of a situation while we solve it?”
