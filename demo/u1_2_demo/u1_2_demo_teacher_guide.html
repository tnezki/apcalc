The Broken Machine

Projected curiosity hook · designed for 5–8 minutes

Time5–8 minutes
FormatWhole-class projection
PurposeBefore notes

Why This Demo Fits Section 1.2

The section focuses on inputs, outputs, rules, and the defining function idea that each input has exactly one output. The demo isolates the repeated-input issue without introducing formal domain/range language.

Materials

  • Projected demo only

Suggested Flow

MinutesPhaseTeacher Focus
0-2Reliable machineHave students predict and verbalize the rule.
2-4Broken machineAsk why input 4 cannot reliably lead to both 9 and 13.
4-6Shared outputLet students debate whether different inputs may share one output.
6-7BridgeAsk for a precise rule that separates the cases.

Expected Student Thinking

Students often overgeneralize “no repeats.” Push them to identify whether the repeated value is an input or an output.

Watch for: treating $-2\rightarrow4$ and $2\rightarrow4$ as automatically invalid.

Transition Into Notes

“What exactly must be predictable about an input-output rule?”
