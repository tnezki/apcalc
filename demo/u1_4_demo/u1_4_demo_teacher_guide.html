The Parentheses Trap

Projected curiosity hook · designed for 5–8 minutes

Time5–8 minutes
FormatWhole-class projection
PurposeBefore notes

Why This Demo Fits Section 1.4

The section emphasizes structure, grouping, equivalent forms, distribution, like terms, and substitution checks. The bag context makes the parentheses carry visible meaning before students manipulate symbols.

Materials

  • Projected demo only
  • Optional three bags or three drawn boxes for a physical version

Suggested Flow

MinutesPhaseTeacher Focus
0-2Tell the storyAsk what one bag contains and what three bags contain.
2-4Choose expressionMake students describe what the 3 multiplies.
4-6Equivalent rewriteInvite a diagram/property/substitution argument.
6-7BridgeAsk when rewriting changes appearance but not value.

Expected Student Thinking

Students should see that $3(x+4)$ means three complete groups. A common error is to multiply only the variable term.

Watch for: “same numbers means same expression.”

Transition Into Notes

“What features of an expression tell us how its parts are grouped and what can be rewritten safely?”
